# Scholarship of Teaching and Learning Ethics Skills

Two Claude skills that support Canadian Scholarship of Teaching and Learning researchers through the Research Ethics Board process. Created by Christine E. Bell, Department of Physiology and Pharmacology, Schulich School of Medicine and Dentistry, Western University.

## The skills

| Skill | What it does | Download |
|---|---|---|
| Recruitment and Consent Materials | Drafts third-party recruitment emails, in-class oral scripts, a recruitment slide, and a Letter of Information and Consent aligned with the Tri-Council Policy Statement (2018). | [sotl-recruitment-consent-materials.zip](https://github.com/drchristinebell/sotl-ethics-skills/raw/main/sotl-recruitment-consent-materials.zip) |
| Ethics Reviewer Response | Produces a response letter, revised application text, and an action item checklist for Research Ethics Board reviewer comments. | [ethics-reviewer-response.zip](https://github.com/drchristinebell/sotl-ethics-skills/raw/main/ethics-reviewer-response.zip) |

## How to install

1. Download the zip file for the skill you want. Do not unzip it.
2. In Claude, open Settings, then Capabilities (or Skills), and upload the zip file.
3. Make sure the skill is turned on, then start a new conversation and describe your task.

## Important

Never enter research participant data into generative artificial intelligence tools. Both skills check for participant data and will ask you to remove it. Always follow your institution's and research team's rules on generative artificial intelligence use.
